# Terms of Use and Service

The Terms live in one place: [`flutter_app/assets/legal/terms.md`](flutter_app/assets/legal/terms.md).
The app shows that file as it is (Menu → Terms of Use, and the link under
"Create Account"), so editing it is how the Terms change - and the date at
its top must change with it.

Before relying on them, have a Kenyan advocate review them. Fill in, at
least:

- the registered name of the business that runs BROKA (section 1 says
  "BROKA");
- a working support address (section 20 says support@broka.co.ke).

The website at broka.co.ke is not built from this repository (only
`/store/*` is, from `web/`), so to publish the Terms there, copy the same
text to the website.
